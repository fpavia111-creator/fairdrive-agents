# Fairdrive pricing (ver 1)

18 Nov 2026. Proposed prices for the product page, in line with the other Fair Agents families
(Fairbooks, Fairhaul, Fairclaim, Fairlease, Fairstay, Fairpractice). Kurt approved using the proposals.

**The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup on your files.** Setup means: we map
your exports (DMS, shop system, estimating system, floorplan and manufacturer statements) to each
agent, set the days, rates, pay plans and limits your store confirms, run every agent on your own
files with you, and leave the reports and the approval routine working on your computer.

Support includes export changes (a DMS upgrade, a new report layout), settings updates when a lender,
manufacturer or state rule changes, new versions of the agents and a monthly check-in.

## What is not included (say it plainly)

- No logins: the agents read files you export; they never connect to a DMS, lender portal, manufacturer
  portal, DMV, insurer or bank.
- No advice: not legal, tax, lending or accounting advice. Days, rates and limits are your settings.
- No compliance verdicts: the OFAC and Red Flags agent checks that records exist and are dated before
  delivery. It does not run checks, read results or say a deal is compliant.
- No actions: nothing is paid, funded, titled, filed, sent or posted by an agent. Payment lists and
  letters are drafts that wait for one named person's click.
- No savings promises: what a store recovers depends on its own files.

## Why these prices

The per-pack prices sit with Fairpractice and Fairstay packs ($1,500 to $3,000). The whole store
($7,500) is lower than the sum of the packs because the exports overlap: one deal file and one
repair-order file feed many agents. The group price reflects that each rooftop still needs its own
mapping and settings. The partner rollout matches the $15,000 ceiling used across the suite.

# Fairdrive Deal Jackets fixtures

Fictional deals at a fictional store, October and November 2026. As of date for the demo and evals: 2026-11-15. Every name is made up.

- `deals.csv`: ten deals. D1005 was unwound and is skipped.
- `documents.csv`: the scanned jacket index. Planted gaps: D1002 (used, financed, with a trade) has no Buyers Guide and no power of attorney, and its credit score notice is unsigned; D1003 (lease) has no lease agreement; D1004's installment contract is unsigned; D1006 (cash, used) has no OFAC or Red Flags record; D1009 has no insurance or licence copy, plus a "We Owe form" the checklist does not know; D1010 has nothing scanned at all. D1008 uses other names for every document (Bill of Sale, RISC, GLBA Privacy Notice and so on) and is complete. D1099 is scanned but is not in the deal file.
